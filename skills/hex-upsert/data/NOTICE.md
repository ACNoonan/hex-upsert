# words.txt.gz

The 50,000 most frequent English words in film and TV subtitles, one `word count` per line,
most frequent first. The gate uses it to decide whether a word is one people ordinarily say.

- Source: `content/2018/en/en_50k.txt` from
  [hermitdave/FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), built from the
  OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus (<http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php>).
